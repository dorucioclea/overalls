# Documentation

Overalls documentation is organized by audience and evidence status. Architecture
records remain under [`architecture/`](architecture/), while the pages below are
current guides unless they are explicitly under `history/`.

## Operations

Deployment, fleet administration, storage, recovery, networking, and service
runbooks are in [`operations/`](operations/README.md).

## Guests and services

Guest image and boot documentation is in [`guests/`](guests/README.md). Service
boundaries and service-specific design notes are in
[`services/`](services/README.md).

## Development and acceptance

Contributor and verification references are in
[`development/`](development/README.md). Live acceptance procedures are in
[`acceptance/`](acceptance/README.md).

## Historical evidence

Recovered plans and implementation ledgers are preserved in
[`history/ledgers/`](history/README.md). They retain their original revision,
scope, and gaps; they are not current runbooks or proof of unrecorded execution.
